Common questions

How do I open Settings?

Tap the gear in the top-right corner. A grown-up check appears first, so only adults can change settings.

How do I set a daily play limit?

In Settings, choose a time under Daily play timer. When time is up, a break screen appears until a grown-up adds more time or tomorrow comes.

How do I turn off sounds or vibration?

In Settings, switch off Sound effects or Vibration.

How do I clear the leaderboard?

In Settings, tap Clear leaderboard.

Does the app collect data or show ads?

No. There are no ads, accounts, or tracking, and nothing leaves the device.
