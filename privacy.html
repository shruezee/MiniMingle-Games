Privacy Policy

MiniMingle Games · Effective October 1, 2026

MiniMingle Games is a game app made for children. We built it so that it does not collect, store, or share any personal information from anyone who uses it, including children.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. It does not connect to the internet and does not send any data to us or to anyone else.

What stays on the device

To work properly, the app saves a few settings only on your device:

  • The hero and play mode your child picked
  • Leaderboard scores (hero name, game, points, and date)
  • Sound, vibration, and daily play-timer settings

This information never leaves the device. Hero names are made-up characters, not your child's name. You can clear scores at any time in Settings, and deleting the app removes everything.

Children's privacy

The app is in Apple's Kids category and follows the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Apple's App Store rules for kids' apps. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The Settings area is protected by a parental gate so that only grown-ups can change it.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking software and has no links that open outside the app.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and the date above.
