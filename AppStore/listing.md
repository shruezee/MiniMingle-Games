## Description (4000)
Five classic games, six cartoon heroes, and loads of fun, made just for kids!

MiniMingle Games brings favorite board and paper games to life with bright, bouncy cartoon style. Choose a game, pick your hero, and play against the computer or pass the device to a friend.

FIVE GAMES TO PLAY
• Tic-Tac-Toe: get three in a row to win
• Connect 4: drop discs and connect four
• Dots & Boxes: close the most boxes
• Bingo: call numbers and fill a line
• Memory Match: find the matching heroes

SIX HEROES TO CHOOSE
Volt Vanguard, Aurora Blaze, Tidecaller, Iron Oak, Shadow Lynx, and Skybolt each have their own colors and emblem, which become their game pieces.

PLAY YOUR WAY
• Play against a friendly computer opponent
• Or play with a friend on the same device
• Climb the leaderboard with every win

MADE FOR FAMILIES
• Daily play timer: grown-ups can set how long kids play each day
• Parental gate protects Settings
• Turn sounds and vibration on or off
• Works on iPhone and iPad, in portrait and landscape
• Works offline, with no internet needed

SAFE FOR KIDS
• No ads
• No in-app purchases
• No accounts or sign-in
• No data collected, and nothing leaves the device
